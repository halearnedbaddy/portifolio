About Me

Get to know me better

I'm the Technical Co-Founder & CTO of Verve Vigor Limited, a multi-product Kenyan startup, where I operate across engineering, infrastructure, and product strategy — from setting up cloud infrastructure and leading stack decisions to driving company registration and governance.

My work centers on production-grade payment infrastructure for African markets: end-to-end Safaricom M-Pesa Daraja integrations (STK Push, C2B, B2C, B2B), USSD/SMS tooling via Africa's Talking, and backend systems built on Spring Boot, Supabase, and AWS. I'm currently completing a BSc in Information Technology at the Technical University of Mombasa.
